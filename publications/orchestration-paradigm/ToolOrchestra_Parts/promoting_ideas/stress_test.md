# Stress Test: ToolOrchestra Promotion Strategy

## Plan Summary
- **Core Action**: Direct cold outreach to high-value targets (CTOs/Founders) using "Career Nuke" strategy.
- **Key Asset**: "The Map" (Agent Architecture Map 2026) used as a calling card/authority signal and "The Audit" (ToolOrchestra series) as technical proof.
- **Value Prop**: Demonstrate unit economics inefficiency in target's current architecture (e.g., "burning cash").
- **Goal**: Invert the funnel—seek 5 high-value conversions (deals/jobs) instead of 50,000 low-value views.

## Must-Be-True Assumptions
| Assumption | How to Verify | Fastest Disproof |
|------------|---------------|------------------|
| **The "Math" holds up** | Peer review of the cost/latency models in Series | A CTO replies pointing out a fundamental error in the "cost-reward function" logic. |
| **Targets read cold email** | Track open rates/reply rates | Send 10 test emails; 0 replies or opens suggests subject lines fail. |
| **"The Map" is novel** | Check existing landscape maps (Sequoia, etc.) | Found a similar map that already defines these terms better. |
| **Targets care about cost** | Research burn rates of AI startups; are they optimizing yet? | Discovery that they are purely focused on growth/capabilities, not cost (yet). |
| **I am credible enough** | Self-audit: Does my profile/series look like a peer or a junior? | A/B test sending as "Researcher" vs "Engineer". |

## Pre-Mortem: It's 1 Year Later, This Failed
| Failure Mode | Warning Signal | Prevention |
|--------------|----------------|------------|
| **"Just Another Sales Pitch"** | Emails ignored or marked spam. No replies. | Make the email fundamentally *useful* without a clear "ask". Value-first. |
| **The "Consultant" Trap** | Replies ask for free advice, no conversion to paid work. | Define clear engagement tiers. "Happy to seek a consulting agreement." |
| **The Map is Ignored** | Low engagement on social sharing of the map. | Ensure visual quality is top-tier (hiring a designer if needed). Needs to look "official". |
| **Technical Rebuttal** | A public takedown of the "ToolOrchestra" theory. | Rigorous peer review before outreach. Soft-launch to friendly experts first. |
| **Wrong Targets** | Reaching marketing/biz dev instead of engineering leads. | Manually verify email addresses and roles. Don't use generic lists. |

## Risk Register
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Fallback |
|------|------------|--------|------------|----------|
| **Reputational Damage** (Being seen as annoying/wrong) | Low | High | Be humble, frame as "discussion" not "correction". | Pivot back to content creation, pause outreach. |
| **Zero Response Rate** | Medium | Medium | Improve copy, change targets, offer different "magnet". | Return to standard content distribution (Reddit/HN). |
| **The Map becomes outdated** | High | Low | Version the map (v1, v2). | Release quarterly updates. |
| **Legal/IP Issues** (using other company names) | Low | Medium | Fair use analysis. Don't imply endorsement. | Remove logos/names if requested. |

## Kill Criteria
- [ ] sent 20 personalized, high-quality emails with 0 positive replies.
- [ ] 3+ experts point out a fatal flaw in the core "ToolOrchestra" technical argument.
- [ ] "The Map" receives universal negative feedback or indifference on initial "soft launch".

## Experiments to Run
- [ ] **The "Map" Teaser Test**: Post a blurry/draft version of "The Map" on Twitter/LinkedIn. **Metric**: >5 DMs or comments asking for the full version.
- [ ] **The "Subject Line" A/B**: Test "Your routing is inefficient" vs "Agent Architecture 2026 Audit". **Metric**: Open rate > 40%.
- [ ] **The "Critique" Request**: Send the series to a neutral expert ask for a "brutal tear-down" before sending to prospects. **Metric**: Positive validation of core claims.
