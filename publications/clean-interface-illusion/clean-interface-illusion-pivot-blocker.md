# Pivot Blocker

## Blocked Candidate

`Capability Is Not Delegation`

## Block Type

`research_grounding_failure`

## Why It Is Blocked

The alphaXiv grounding pass did not support opening a new flagship object around delegability.

The seam was strong enough to justify continued research, but not strong enough to justify a pivot.

## Evidence Used In The Block

The grounding pass combined targeted search, paper-level abstract review, and assistant pressure-testing across this seam:

- `2602.16666` `Towards a Science of AI Agent Reliability`
- `2602.11865` `Intelligent AI Delegation`
- `2604.04918` `Comparing Human Oversight Strategies for Computer-Use Agents`
- `2505.18066` `Towards Uncertainty Aware Task Delegation and Human-AI Collaborative Decision-Making`
- `2401.04729` `Human Delegation Behavior in Human-AI Collaboration`
- `2602.06948` `Agentic Uncertainty Reveals Agentic Overconfidence`
- `2505.09875` `Characterizing Unintended Consequences in Human-GUI Agent Collaboration for Web Browsing`
- `2504.17934` `Toward a Human-Centered Evaluation Framework for Trustworthy LLM-Powered GUI Agents`
- `2603.10708` `Believing vs. Achieving -- The Disconnect between Efficacy Beliefs and Collaborative Outcomes`

## Blocking Findings

1. The seam remains too correlational.
2. The seam does not yet connect the proposed mechanism to consequential delegation outcomes with enough force.
3. The seam does not establish a principled threshold for when reliability and calibration are good enough for delegation.
4. The best-supported outputs from the current seam are a narrower synthesis around appropriate reliance or a descriptive account of reliability noise, not a true north-star delegability essay.

## What Would Unblock It

At least one of these would have to arrive:

- field or deployment evidence tying miscalibration to measurable harm or opportunity cost
- intervention evidence showing that reducing the proposed mechanism changes consequential delegation outcomes
- a principled delegation threshold grounded in evidence rather than slogan

## Workflow Consequence

Do not treat `Capability Is Not Delegation` as the next pivot.

It may remain in the candidate pool, but only as a blocked hypothesis.
