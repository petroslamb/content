## Working Thesis

Human-AI collaboration should be judged not by model accuracy alone but by team readiness: a dynamic state visible in interaction traces that captures calibration, error recovery, safety signals, and governance capacity.

## What Must Be Proven

This candidate only survives if the next research pass can strengthen at least one of these three gaps:

1. `Readiness validation against real outcomes`
   Do readiness-style metrics predict anything consequential in deployment or realistic collaboration settings?

2. `Error-signature-specific intervention matching`
   Can the literature connect specific failure patterns to specific intervention types, rather than treating all reliance failures as one problem?

3. `Outcome improvement, not just exposure change`
   Are there cases where human intervention or interface design measurably improves downstream outcomes rather than merely changing when users notice problems?
