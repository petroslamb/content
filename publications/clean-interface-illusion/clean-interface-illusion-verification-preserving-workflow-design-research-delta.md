## Core Thesis

The relevant workflow question is not whether a human remains nominally in the loop. It is whether the workflow preserves genuine opportunities to challenge, override, and repair AI outputs before commitment.

## Why It Rose

1. `Verification boundary papers` show that explanations often fail because tasks are hard to verify, not just because users are inattentive.
2. `Independent aggregation papers` show that preserving independent judgments can outperform sequential AI-advice workflows.
3. `Workflow papers` show concrete designs that decouple roles, preserve provenance, or add structured oversight in expert settings.
4. `Contestability papers` add a formal vocabulary and early measurable proxies for challenge quality.
