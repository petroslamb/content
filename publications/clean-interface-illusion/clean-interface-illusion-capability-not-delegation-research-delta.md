# Capability Is Not Delegation: Research Delta

## Status

This brief is now a provisional research surface, not an approved pivot brief.

The later grounding review in [`clean-interface-illusion-pivot-grounding-review.md`](clean-interface-illusion-pivot-grounding-review.md) concluded that this seam is promising but not yet strong enough to justify a flagship pivot.

## Purpose

This brief defines the targeted research restart that was required to test a possible flagship object.

The job is not broad rediscovery. The job is to test whether the donor essay's best assets can support a stronger thesis about delegability.

## Provisional Pivot

- Public object: `Capability Is Not Delegation`
- Internal concept: `the delegability gap`
- Donor package: `clean-interface-illusion`

## Reusable Evidence From The Donor Package

- `tau-bench` for repeated-run instability and score reinterpretation
- `WebSuite` for failure localization
- `WebForge` for realism without regression to toy-benchmark critique
- `Towards a Science of AI Agent Reliability` for consistency, robustness, predictability, and safety as separate variables
- `LiveMCPBench` and `TheAgentCompany` for toolscale and consequential-task pressure

## Missing Seams

The donor package is strong on capability-versus-trust interpretation. It is weaker on the actual mechanics of delegation.

The research delta must test four seams:

1. `Delegation structure`
   Can the literature define delegation as more than "use the model more carefully"?
2. `Legible oversight`
   What makes risky moments visible in time for meaningful intervention?
3. `Uncertainty-aware handoff`
   What signals help humans decide whether to delegate, retain control, or intervene?
4. `Containment and failure cost`
   What happens when unreliable agents, weak signals, and poor supervision interact in consequential settings?

## Initial Shortlist From The First alphaXiv Pass

### Direct delegation and collaboration

- `2602.11865` `Intelligent AI Delegation`
  Relevance: explicit delegation framework with authority, accountability, boundaries, and trust.
- `2401.04729` `Human Delegation Behavior in Human-AI Collaboration`
  Relevance: contextual information changes delegation behavior and improves team performance.
- `2505.18066` `Towards Uncertainty Aware Task Delegation and Human-AI Collaborative Decision-Making`
  Relevance: uncertainty signaling changes correct reliance behavior.
- `2603.11011` `Task-Aware Delegation Cues for LLM Agents`
  Relevance: explicit collaboration signaling layer for visible and auditable delegation decisions.

### Oversight and supervision structure

- `2604.04918` `Comparing Human Oversight Strategies for Computer-Use Agents`
  Relevance: intervention success depends on whether risky moments become legible during execution.
- `2507.03525` `Limits of Safe AI Deployment: Differentiating Oversight and Control`
  Relevance: separates prevention-oriented control from ex-post oversight and names where meaningful supervision fails.
- `2508.03858` `MI9: An Integrated Runtime Governance Framework for Agentic AI`
  Relevance: runtime control and containment machinery for agent deployment.

### Consequential harm and guarded execution

- `2604.09155` `CORA: Conformal Risk-Controlled Agents for Safeguarded Mobile GUI Automation`
  Relevance: selective execution and abstention under explicit risk budgets.
- `2506.00618` `RiOSWorld: Benchmarking the Risk of Multimodal Computer-Use Agents`
  Relevance: real-world computer-use risk evaluation with user-originated and environmental risks.

## What The alphaXiv Assistant Added

The first assistant pass pressured the pivot in a useful way:

- it preferred `The Delegability Gap` as the strongest raw concept
- it argued that `Capability Is Not Delegation` works best only if it is not framed as a user-education slogan
- it identified the missing seam as empirical evidence on failure cascades or consequential failure cost

That means the research delta should treat delegability as a structural system property, not a behavioral complaint about users.

## Research Questions

1. What makes a task delegable rather than merely solvable?
2. Which variables recur across delegation, oversight, and reliability papers?
3. What evidence distinguishes bounded usefulness from trustworthy handoff?
4. Which interventions reduce user burden without pretending supervision is free?
5. Is there a clean failure-containment seam strong enough to give the next essay urgency?

## Non-Goals

- Do not restart broad benchmark discovery.
- Do not widen into general human-AI collaboration literature without a direct delegation or oversight payoff.
- Do not turn the next essay into a generic safety-governance survey.

## Exit Criteria

Proceed to a new flagship package only if a later research pass yields:

- a thesis that clearly exceeds the donor essay's aperture
- at least one body section not centered on benchmark design
- a portable doctrine about handoff, oversight, routing, or abstention

Abort or narrow the pivot if the literature only supports:

- "people need better trust calibration"
- "supervision matters"
- "evaluation influences deployment"

Those are true but not yet flagship-level breakout objects.
