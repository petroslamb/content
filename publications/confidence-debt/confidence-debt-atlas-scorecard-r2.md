# Atlas Live-Draft Scorecard — R2

## Changes From R1

The five surgical fixes from the external review:

1. **Opening split**: The contradiction now lands in a short standalone paragraph before the analytical frame begins. The first paragraph sets the scene. The second paragraph delivers the punch. The third paragraph begins the analysis.

2. **Transfer-mechanism passage**: New material in The Cascade section. Three sub-paragraphs name the specific handoff at each boundary: artifact-to-evaluation (score inherits unverified confidence), evaluation-to-human (score determines deployment and oversight allocation), human-to-pipeline (thinned correction capacity oversees the next round of outputs). This is followed by an anchoring paragraph connecting the transfer to independently documented multi-stage automation-bias dynamics.

3. **Limits repositioned**: Limits now sits above the final closing paragraph, separated by a horizontal rule. The rhetorical ending ("the capacity to push back has been quietly spent") is now the last thing the reader encounters.

4. **Doctrine expanded**: The three-layer discipline is now broken into bolded layer-specific rules with enough operational detail to be auditable. Evidence hierarchy, interpretive discipline, correction-capacity preservation.

5. **Subtitle added**: "What your AI workflow borrows when the output moves faster than the evidence."

User-requested changes: subheadings and inline (Name et al.) citations are now present throughout.

## What Improved

**Cascade concreteness**: The transfer-mechanism passage is the largest structural improvement. R1 stated that the layers compose a pipeline. R2 names the specific handoff at each boundary and explains what gets transferred (inherited confidence), what gets lost (adjudication, caveats, correction capacity), and why the handoff is structurally forced rather than coincidental. The automation-bias anchoring paragraph connects this to independently documented dynamics where intermediate acceptance compounds rather than being caught at later review points.

**Opening pace**: The split works. The contradiction lands faster. The analytical frame starts from a position of established tension rather than needing to create it.

**Ending structure**: Moving Limits before the final paragraph resolves the external reviewer's concern about the rhetorical landing arriving too early. The reader now encounters the honest caveats and then receives the closing statement, which means the essay's last impression is its strongest sentence rather than a concession.

**Operational utility**: The three-layer doctrine is now specific enough that a team lead could use it as an audit checklist without referring back to the essay's argument.

## What Did Not Improve

**Evaluation section energy**: The evaluation section (How Confidence Gets Packaged) is improved at the structural level (the transfer-mechanism passage at the end does heavy lifting) but the tau-bench and WebSuite/WebForge passages in the middle are still inherited from the clean-interface-illusion donor. They are polished and functional but not reimagined for maximum atmospheric pressure. This remains the essay's lowest-energy zone.

**Debate temperature**: The essay is still sober rather than explosive. The voice guide demands intellectual calm, and the essay delivers it. Whether sobriety is a ceiling or a strength depends on the reviewer's framework. The debate energy subscore is likely still 7-8.
