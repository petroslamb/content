# Consolidated Blueprint: Evidence Governance

**Thesis**: The bottleneck of AI deployment in high-stakes environments is no longer generation capability, but the "Honesty Gap"—the misalignment between fluent model confidence and verifiable evidence. bridging this gap requires an "Evidence Governance" pattern that makes errors findable and trust auditable.

## Core Lenses

1. **The Honesty Gap**: The decoupling of confidence and correctness (Simhi et al., 2025).
2. **The Verification Bottleneck**: As generation costs drop to zero, verification effort becomes the dominant economic constraint (Rasheed et al., 2026).
3. **The Evidence Hierarchy**: Borrowing from medicine: T1 (Raw artifacts) > T2 (Validation docs) > T3 (Narrative summaries).
4. **Inspectable Mediocrity**: Success isn't "AI is always right"; success is "AI wrongness is findable and manageable."

## Argument Architecture

### 1. Opening: The Moment of Contradiction
- Re-use the "CTR-001" anecdote from the flagship draft.
- The failure isn't the model error; it's the lack of a rule for resolving it.
- **Hook**: "Generation is cheap. Trust is expensive. The expense isn't compute — it's honesty."

### 2. The Finding: Why Better Models Aren't Enough
- Draw from the Ethereum project (EIP-verify).
- Finding: Auditability improved faster than correctness.
- Contrast Haiku vs Opus: Haiku is triage-grade; Opus is adjudication-grade.
- The "Scaffold of Mediocrity": The AI is good at building the tools to catch its own mistakes.

### 3. The Pattern: Five Components of Governance
- **Evidence Hierarchy**: Mechanical tie-break rules.
- **Scoring Rubric**: multi-dimensional scoring (fidelity, location, flow, etc.).
- **Contradiction Register**: Disagreements as first-class data.
- **Claim Ledger**: Release gating (no evidence, no claim).
- **Iteration Loop**: The dual-gate loop (Contract A -> Artifact B).

### 4. Convergence: Evidence-Based AI
- Anchor in history: Medicine’s 1990s evidence crisis.
- The shift from "Expert Opinion" to "Systematic Review."
- The "Audit Card" as the new reporting standard.

### 5. Closing: The Human Discipline
- The meta-irony: Scaffolding built with AI.
- The real challenge: Human willingness to downgrade claims.
- Final call to action: "If you ship AI analysis without a contradiction register, start one this week."
