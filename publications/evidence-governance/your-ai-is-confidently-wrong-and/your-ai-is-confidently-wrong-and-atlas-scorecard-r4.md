# Atlas Scorecard

## Whole-Draft Rationale

Revision 4 is a materially better object than the doctrine-leaning draft because it stops asking one project to certify a general standard. The bounded case-study move raises precision, improves trust, and gives the essay a cleaner governing claim. The tradeoff is that the piece now wins more on honesty and structure than on force. It is publishable in this form, but it is not yet an outstanding flagship because the opening scene is still more described than dramatized and the middle still carries a fair amount of explanatory burden.

## Opening Anchor

> One `eip-verify` run produced two outputs that should never have been allowed to sit beside each other without consequence.

This is the right opening move for the case-study version. It establishes conflict inside one concrete system and gives the essay a real object to examine. It still needs one sharper sensory or procedural detail to become fully memorable.

## Middle Anchor

> The five-component frame came later. It was not the starting insight. It was the name given afterward to the controls the project had to invent in order to keep the outputs from governing themselves.

This is the essay's best sentence-level correction. It rescues the framework from sounding like prefabricated doctrine and makes it feel earned by project failure.

## Ending Anchor

> That is why the real cost in this case was not compute. It was adjudication discipline.

The ending lands cleanly because it returns the essay to a human operating cost rather than a capability claim. It is less grand than the doctrine version and more credible for that reason.

## Traction

## Craft

**7**. The prose is controlled and the structure is sound. The main craft limitation is density in the middle and an opening that still reads slightly more like an executive diagnosis than a scene.

## Publication Power

**7**. This is good enough to publish as a serious essay. It is not yet the package's flagship ceiling because it still lacks one unmistakable coined concept, one harder narrative scene, and one degree more compression in the body.

## Next-score Blocker

To move this above a 7-tier essay, the next revision has to improve force without expanding the claim again. The most direct path is a sharper contradiction scene up front, a leaner empirical center, and one more distinctive phrase or concept that readers will carry away from the piece.
