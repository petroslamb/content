# Publication Route Note

## Route Recommendation

Use a fast-feedback ladder:

1. AIware-style feedback sprint only after draftability clears the route.
2. Software-engineering workshop or ICSE NIER-style short paper after method
   hardening.
3. arXiv only after a complete manuscript, references, method section, artifact
   availability statement, and venue/preprint checklist exist.

## Current Decision

Do not submit, post, or prepare an external system mutation in this proof run.
The route is not ready for manuscript drafting until the case-study method is
hardened and a docs-only two-reviewer gate approves the same drafting route.

## Fit Rationale

The strongest scholarly framing is software-engineering infrastructure for
governed agent workflows. The paper should be reviewed as a framework and
artifact-analysis paper, not as a benchmark paper and not as a publication
automation claim.

## Checklist Before Any Posting

- complete manuscript draft
- citation-grade references
- method section with unit, corpus, coding scheme, observations, and non-claims
- artifact availability language for retained local proof evidence
- venue or arXiv policy checklist
- approval record that does not treat arXiv as peer review
