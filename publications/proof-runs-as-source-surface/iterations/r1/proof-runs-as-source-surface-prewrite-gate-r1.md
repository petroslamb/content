## Promise

The candidate is authorized to argue that governed proof runs are the source surface humans can review when autonomous content work asks to initialize, mutate, resume, or move a package forward. The essay must keep the claim local and staged: the current proof supports governed local lifecycle movement through recorded gates, not public release reliability.

## Spine

1. Start with the failure of a raw completion transcript as proof.
2. Define the proof run object through Phase 2's source, runtime, evidence, validation, and human-view layers.
3. Show what the local proof emitted and why the gate stop matters.
4. Use the research shortlist to explain why specification discipline, context grounding, schema gates, and audit surfaces are the right backdrop.
5. End on the remaining boundary: publication reliability needs later governed stages.

## Paragraph burden

The opening must earn the distinction between activity and evidence. The architecture section must explain the layers without sounding like README prose. The proof section must show the reader a concrete trail from workflow contract to gate record. The research section must connect the papers to the local architecture with visible caveats. The close must prevent overclaiming by naming what this proof still does not cover.

## Evidence burden

The local proof artifacts must do body work for the repo-specific claim. The claim-boundary ledger and paper index govern paper use. SWE-AGI, Spec Kit Agents, SWE-Bench Pro, the Productivity-Reliability Paradox, Talk Freely, Execute Strictly, and SPECA may support the broader research frame, but the draft must label the bridge from those papers to content proof runs as synthesis.

## Cut order

Cut command-by-command narration first, then secondary paper detail, then any release-stage speculation. Do not cut the gate boundary, the source-surface definition, or the limitation that external mutation safety is still unproven.

## Failure triggers

Void the draft pass if it claims autonomous publication is solved, describes `content-agent` as inventing workflow behavior, collapses `status.yaml` into the whole architecture, or treats paper citations as proof of the local runtime rather than context for why the runtime shape matters.
