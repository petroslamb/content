## Disagreement And Uncertainty

The main uncertainty is how much of the threat shift is Mythos-specific versus part of a broader class of AI-assisted offensive capability. The safer language is "Mythos-class" unless a claim is verified against Anthropic or another primary source.

There is also a governance tension between enabling defensive AI and preventing unmanaged AI use. The session should feed separate follow-ups for technical controls and board-level risk translation.
