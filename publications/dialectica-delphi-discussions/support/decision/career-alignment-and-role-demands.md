# Career Alignment And Role Demands

This note reframes the Dialectica Delphi opportunity as a career decision, not only an offer decision.

The question is not whether Petros can do the work. The question is whether the work is senior enough, strategic enough, and bounded enough to justify committing at this career stage.

## Career Context

Petros's current career direction, based on the career workspace and 2026 CV, is already moving away from commodity development:

- independent systems consultant
- production AI and agentic systems
- architecture beyond prototype
- evaluation, reliability, and failure-mode analysis
- cross-functional leadership between research, product, and engineering
- meaningful work with enough freedom, stability, and family-compatible rhythm

At 45, the next move should not be another blind leap into a company where the title says senior but the work becomes tickets, firefighting, and vague accountability.

The right next move should consolidate a new professional identity:

**AI systems architect and strategic technical lead for ambiguous, high-value operating systems.**

The Dialectica opportunity is only aligned if it supports that identity.

## How This Role Could Align

The role can align strongly if Delphi is treated as a new operating system inside Dialectica:

- expert community strategy
- AI-assisted insight extraction
- data model for expert participation
- transcript, chat, and contribution processing
- journey orchestration and give/get loops
- anonymized content and client intelligence
- build-versus-buy architecture
- compliance-aware AI workflow design
- measurable operating leverage

That is meaningful work. It sits between strategy, architecture, AI, product, and organizational design.

It also fits Petros's CV: production AI, SaaS architecture, NLP, data systems, executive communication, stakeholder alignment, and turning fragile prototypes into deployable systems.

## How This Role Could Fail

The role is misaligned if Dialectica wants:

- a senior engineer to implement Pere's ideas without shaping them
- a platform build before discovery
- a member portal as the first milestone
- vague ownership without authority
- AI summaries without compliance ownership
- accountability for community success without control over product, data, or operations
- a role evaluated by output volume instead of system leverage
- standard dev expectations under a strategic job description

That would repeat the pattern to avoid: entering a company on hope, then discovering that the role is narrower, less empowered, and less meaningful than promised.

## The Shift In Posture

Do not approach Pere and Michael as if you are asking them to define the role.

Approach them with a proposed role charter.

The negotiation stance is:

**"This is the kind of role I am willing to consider. If this matches what Dialectica needs, let's discuss it seriously. If not, it is better to know now."**

That puts the conversation on Petros's terms.

## The Role To Ask For

Ask for a role closer to:

**Principal Technical Architect / Founding Technical Lead, Delphi Expert Network**

Alternative titles:

- Principal AI Systems Architect, Delphi
- Technical Product Architect, Delphi Expert Network
- Founding Technical Lead, Expert Community Systems
- Staff/Principal Engineer, AI Expert Network Platform

Avoid a plain "Senior Engineer" title unless the written scope clearly carries Staff-plus authority.

## The Core Demand

The core ask is not a list of questions. It is a charter:

Petros leads technical discovery and architecture for Delphi as a new expert-community operating system. Pere owns product/community direction. Michael or another named sponsor owns technical evaluation and access. Compliance owns consent and reuse boundaries. The first milestone is a paid 30-45 day discovery and prototype phase, not a full platform launch.

## Non-Negotiable Demands

### 1. Senior Mandate

Ask for explicit ownership of technical discovery and architecture.

This includes:

- operating-loop design
- data model
- AI workflow architecture
- transcript/chat processing approach
- build-versus-buy recommendations
- system integration plan
- success metrics
- technical risk register

Do not accept a role where architecture is already decided and Petros is brought in to implement.

### 2. Paid Discovery Phase Before Commitment To Platform

Ask for the first phase to be 30-45 days of paid discovery and thin prototyping.

Deliverables:

- current-system map
- expert journey map
- source-data inventory
- consent and compliance map
- first data model
- build-versus-buy recommendation
- AI processing and review workflow
- post-session operator workflow prototype
- metrics and baseline plan
- phase-two recommendation

This is the strongest way to avoid a blind leap.

### 3. Named Sponsor And Evaluator

Ask who owns sponsorship and evaluation.

The preferred answer:

- Pere owns product/community direction.
- Michael owns or co-owns the technical sponsorship/evaluation.
- Engineering/data/compliance owners are named.

If no one can say who evaluates success, the role is dangerous.

### 4. Decision Rights

Ask for written decision rights.

Petros should have authority to recommend:

- not building a full platform yet
- buying tools instead of building them
- keeping AI workflows operator-reviewed
- delaying automation until consent and quality are clear
- changing scope based on discovery

Without this, the role has responsibility without leverage.

### 5. Access To Real Systems

Ask for access in the first month to:

- expert profile data
- CRM or expert-management data
- consultation history
- email/journey tooling
- Zoom attendance, transcript, and chat exports
- existing dbt or analytics models
- content workflows
- compliance constraints
- current engineering architecture

No access means no serious architecture.

### 6. Compliance Partnership

Ask for a named compliance/legal owner.

The system touches personal data, inferred expertise, transcripts, chat, AI processing, client-facing reuse, and public content. Engineering should design with compliance, not silently absorb legal risk.

### 7. Resource Commitment

Ask what resources attach to the first phase.

Minimum:

- time with Pere
- time with Michael or technical sponsor
- data/system access
- one engineering/data contact
- one compliance contact
- one operator/content contact

For phase two, ask whether there is budget for design, engineering, data, AI tooling, and external vendors.

### 8. Career-Level Title And Compensation

Ask for title and compensation to match the scope.

If the work is strategic, cross-functional, and architecture-led, it should not be packaged as ordinary senior IC compensation.

Do not let an exciting AI/community mission become a discount on seniority.

### 9. Work Model And Life Boundary

Ask for a work model compatible with the next five years, not only the next quarter.

This should cover:

- remote/hybrid expectations
- travel expectations
- meeting load
- on-call or incident expectations
- availability windows
- focus time for architecture and writing
- family-compatible rhythm

The personal north star is freedom, health, family, financial resilience, and meaningful work. The role should support that, not consume it.

### 10. Public-Safe Career Asset

Ask whether Petros can eventually create public-safe thought leadership from the work, with approval.

Not raw Dialectica data. Not confidential participant material.

But possibly:

- anonymized operating-model lessons
- AI workflow architecture patterns
- expert-community design principles
- public-safe articles or talks reviewed by Dialectica

This matters because the role should build Petros's long-term career capital, not disappear inside confidential internal work.

## Preferred Offer Structure

The cleanest structure may be:

**Phase 1: Paid strategic discovery engagement**

- 30-45 days
- clear deliverables
- access agreed upfront
- sponsor and evaluator named
- ends with written phase-two recommendation

**Phase 2: Convert to senior role only if the charter is confirmed**

- title reflects Principal/Staff-level scope
- reporting line and sponsor are written
- decision rights are named
- resources and budget are attached
- success metrics are agreed

This structure protects both sides. Dialectica gets clarity before committing to a large build. Petros avoids committing to an undefined role.

## What To Say To Pere And Michael

Use this as the direct positioning:

```text
I am interested in this because it sits at the intersection of AI systems, expert networks, content, data, and product strategy. That is the kind of work I want to do next.

But I do not want to make another blind leap into an undefined senior engineering role. At this point in my career, I am looking for strategic technical ownership: architecture, discovery, operating-model design, and leadership across product, data, AI, and compliance.

So I would like to discuss this as a proposed charter:

I lead technical discovery and architecture for Delphi as an expert-community operating system. Pere owns product/community direction. Michael or another named sponsor defines the technical evaluation bar and unlocks access. Compliance owns reuse and consent boundaries.

The first milestone should be a paid 30-45 day discovery and thin prototype phase, not a commitment to build a full platform. The output would be a system map, expert journey map, data inventory, consent map, first data model, build-versus-buy recommendation, AI review workflow, and a small operator prototype around one or more Delphi sessions.

If that is the kind of role Dialectica wants, I think there is a serious conversation to have. If the need is mainly senior implementation for a pre-decided platform, it is probably not the right move for me.
```

## What To Send Before The Meeting

Short version:

```text
Before we go deeper, I want to frame the kind of role I would seriously consider.

I am interested in Delphi because it combines AI systems, expert networks, content, data, and product strategy. But I would not want to join as a standard senior engineer for an undefined platform build.

The role that makes sense to me is technical ownership of the Delphi operating loop: discovery, architecture, data model, AI workflow, build-versus-buy, and a first operator prototype. Pere would own product/community direction; Michael or another named sponsor would own technical sponsorship/evaluation; compliance would own consent and reuse boundaries.

My preferred first step would be a paid 30-45 day discovery and prototype phase before committing to a larger platform or permanent role. I would like us to discuss whether that matches what Dialectica actually needs.
```

## Decision Rule

Accept serious negotiation if they engage with the charter.

Continue discovery if they like the idea but cannot name sponsor, access, decision rights, or first milestone.

Decline or step back if they reframe the role as ordinary senior engineering delivery with strategic expectations attached.

The right answer from them is not immediate agreement to every term. The right answer is that they discuss the role at the right level: mandate, sponsorship, access, authority, resources, and first-phase design.
