# Dialectica Company Model

This note captures the working model for the publication. It should be updated as more first-hand observations, discussion transcripts, and commercial details are added.

The immediate operating counterpart is Pere Molisn Ayuso, referred to as Pere in the package. Pere is organizing Delphi Zoom sessions with experts and attendees from Dialectica's expert pools and is exploring how those sessions can support community creation and content creation.

## Public Baseline

Dialectica describes itself as an information services company that connects business professionals with real-time insights from experts. Its public materials emphasize expert access, custom sourcing, knowledge infrastructure, and AI-enabled insight workflows.

Relevant public signals:

- Dialectica positions itself around "unique, real-time insights" for business decision-making. Source: [Dialectica About](https://www.dialectica.io/about)
- Its expert-facing surface frames participation as knowledge sharing, networking, and consulting engagements. Source: [Dialectica Experts](https://www.dialectica.io/experts)
- Its FAQ describes clients as typically investment funds, consulting firms, and corporate strategy teams looking for subject-matter expertise. Source: [Dialectica FAQ](https://www.dialectica.io/faq)
- The platform messaging points toward proprietary insight infrastructure, including a knowledge graph and GenAI tooling. Source: [Dialectica Platform](https://www.dialectica.io/)

## Current Model

The standard expert-network model is transaction-heavy:

- a client has a question
- the network sources relevant experts
- experts are screened for fit and compliance
- the client pays for access to calls, surveys, or related insight products
- the network captures operational margin from matching, compliance, scheduling, and synthesis

The expert's main value is access to paid engagements. The client's main value is faster access to situated knowledge. The company's defensibility comes from expert supply, sourcing speed, client relationships, workflow quality, compliance, and accumulated knowledge infrastructure.

## Strategic Extension

The Dialectica Delphi discussion format suggests a stronger layer: live expert networks around important topics.

Instead of treating experts only as individually callable sources, Dialectica can create recurring arenas where experts interact with each other around live questions. The commercial asset then becomes not only the expert list, but the structured record of expert disagreement, convergence, confidence, and revision over time.

Potential value to experts:

- visibility with serious peers
- access to a higher-quality professional conversation than public social media
- feedback on their own assumptions
- status from being invited into a curated arena
- paid or reputation-linked opportunities that follow from demonstrated judgment

Potential value to Dialectica:

- richer expert profiles based on observed reasoning, not only credentials
- reusable topic maps and claims generated from live discussion
- early detection of experts who are unusually strong, current, or contrarian
- a proprietary discussion graph connecting experts, topics, claims, uncertainty, and client demand
- differentiated insight products beyond one-off calls

Potential value to clients:

- faster sensemaking on emergent topics
- explicit disagreement maps rather than single-expert anecdotes
- better calibration of confidence and uncertainty
- longitudinal tracking of how expert views change as facts move
- access to synthesized intelligence without needing to sit through every conversation

## Reference Company Patterns

Boardy suggests the intro layer: a network becomes more valuable when it can remember enough about people to make timely, high-signal introductions. Dialectica can adapt that pattern by using Delphi attendance, questions, and expert claims to propose selective expert-to-expert and expert-to-client follow-ups.

World 50 suggests the premium community layer: the product is not only content, but confidential access to a group whose members learn from each other. For Dialectica, the lesson is that community value comes from curation, small-group structure, trust rules, serious moderation, and programming around current problems.

CEB suggests the research-product layer: repeated member conversations can become briefs, tools, databases, and decision support. For Dialectica, the opportunity is to convert Delphi discussions into aggregated insight products while keeping raw participant disclosure protected.

## Hormuz As First Case

The Hormuz fallout discussion is useful because the topic is live, cross-disciplinary, and commercially relevant. It touches geopolitics, shipping, energy, insurance, military risk, financial markets, and regional strategy.

That makes it a good test case for the proposed model:

- a single expert call may be useful, but a structured discussion can reveal which assumptions experts share
- disagreement is valuable because it shows where uncertainty lives
- chat participation can surface hidden demand, follow-up questions, and participant geography
- the transcript can become a claim map, scenario map, and expert-signal map

## Open Questions

- What moderation format produces useful disagreement rather than panel noise?
- What data model best captures experts, claims, uncertainty, evidence, and revisions?
- How can the company avoid extracting too much free value from experts while still creating reusable insight assets?
