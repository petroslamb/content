# Decision Brief

This is the top-level surface for deciding how to engage Pere and Michael about the Dialectica Delphi role.

The stance is simple: I am not asking them to define the role. I am proposing the role I would seriously consider.

## The Decision

This is a career decision, not just an offer decision.

The good version is strategic technical ownership of a new expert-community operating system: AI, data, content, expert participation, workflow architecture, compliance boundaries, and measurable operating leverage.

The bad version is another vague senior engineering leap: high responsibility, unclear authority, and a platform build that has not earned its own scope.

The question is whether Dialectica is willing to discuss the role at the level of mandate, sponsorship, access, authority, resources, and first-phase design.

## The Role I Want

The role should be closer to Principal Technical Architect or Founding Technical Lead for the Delphi Expert Network, not ordinary senior developer delivery.

In the strategic version, I own the architecture of the problem. In the senior engineer version, I inherit the problem and build around it.

| Layer | Strategic Technical Ownership | Senior Engineer Delivery |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pere | Owns the community/product bet: who Delphi serves, what experts get, what loops matter, and what outcomes are worth pursuing. | Gives product ideas, requests features, changes priorities, and expects engineering to build around them. |
| Petros | Owns the technical operating system: data model, AI workflow, build-versus-buy, first prototype, integration plan, and proof that Delphi compounds. | Owns implementation: screens, APIs, integrations, dashboards, automations, and fixes after others define the system. |
| Michael | Owns sponsorship and technical legitimacy: access, technical bar, core-IP judgment, authority, and phase-one evaluation. | Comments from a distance without formally sponsoring, unblocking, or evaluating the mandate. |
| Compliance | Named partner owning consent, reuse, retention, anonymization, and client-facing boundaries. | Vague constraint that becomes hidden engineering risk. |
| First phase | Paid 30-45 day discovery/prototype before any platform commitment. | Start building the platform while discovery happens informally. |
| Success | Prove a repeatable expert-community operating loop. | Ship requested platform/features. |

This aligns with the career direction I want now: AI systems, architecture, strategy, and leadership around ambiguous but valuable operating systems.

## What I Need To Get Out Of This

This role only makes sense if it compounds my own career direction, not only Dialectica's platform idea.

The role should strengthen my positioning as an AI systems architect and strategic technical lead: someone who turns ambiguous AI/product bets into reliable operating systems, not someone who absorbs vague delivery work.

It should create real career capital:

- a senior mandate I can credibly describe later, even if details remain confidential
- experience owning the architecture of an expert-network AI operating loop
- executive-facing work with Pere, Michael, compliance, operators, and engineering
- evidence of discovery, architecture, AI workflow design, and measurable operating leverage
- public-safe lessons or thought leadership with Dialectica approval, without exposing confidential expert or client material

It should also support the life direction I am protecting: health, family, financial resilience, and focused high-leverage work. A good role gives me strategic depth and stability. A bad role consumes calendar, attention, and confidence while calling ordinary delivery "ownership."

The question is not only whether the work is interesting. The question is whether it leaves me more senior, more credible, more independent, and better positioned for the next move.

## The Deal I Put On The Table

Petros leads technical discovery and architecture for Delphi as an expert-community operating system. Pere owns product/community direction. Michael or another named sponsor owns technical evaluation and access. Compliance owns consent and reuse boundaries. First milestone is a paid 30-45 day discovery and prototype phase, not a full platform launch.

The first phase should produce a system map, expert journey map, source-data inventory, consent map, first data model, build-versus-buy recommendation, AI review workflow, and one thin operator prototype around real Delphi sessions.

Phase two should only be discussed after that evidence exists.

## My Non-Negotiables

The role needs a named sponsor and evaluator. If Michael is the sponsor, say so. If not, name the person.

It needs real decision rights: architecture, data model, AI workflow, build-versus-buy, and whether to delay a full platform.

It needs access in the first month to expert data, CRM or expert-management data, email/journey tooling, Zoom exports, transcripts, chat, dbt or analytics models, and compliance constraints.

It needs a named compliance/legal partner. Transcript reuse, inferred expertise, AI processing, anonymization, retention, and client-facing outputs cannot be informal engineering risk.

It needs resources: time with Pere, technical sponsor access, a data/engineering contact, a compliance contact, and an operator/content contact.

It needs title, compensation, and work model to match the scope. This should support strategy, leadership, health, family, and focused architecture work, not collapse into standard dev throughput.

It needs career-capital upside. If the work must remain fully invisible, the title, compensation, authority, and internal sponsorship need to be strong enough to compensate for that. Prefer a written path to public-safe artifacts or lessons with approval.

## Price Anchor

For standard senior engineering delivery, this is a different conversation. That work sits closer to local senior-engineer compensation.

For this charter, I should ask for Principal/Staff-level scope and compensation:

- paid discovery phase: EUR 20k-35k for 30-45 days, or roughly EUR 900-1,200/day
- permanent role: EUR 100k-130k base, depending on title, authority, upside, and written scope
- minimum permanent threshold: EUR 90k only if the title, sponsor, decision rights, discovery phase, and upside are strong

If they want strategic accountability at senior-engineer pricing, the role is mis-scoped.

## What I Send Them

```text
Before we go deeper, I want to frame the kind of role I would seriously consider.

I am interested in Delphi because it combines AI systems, expert networks, content, data, and product strategy. But I do not want to join as a standard senior engineer for an undefined platform build.

The role that makes sense to me is technical ownership of the Delphi operating loop: discovery, architecture, data model, AI workflow, build-versus-buy, and a first operator prototype. Pere would own product/community direction; Michael or another named sponsor would own technical sponsorship and evaluation; compliance would own consent and reuse boundaries.

My preferred first step would be a paid 30-45 day discovery and prototype phase before committing to a larger platform or permanent role. I would like us to discuss whether that matches what Dialectica actually needs.
```

In simple terms, Pere owns the community and product bet. He decides what Delphi should become for experts, attendees, and Dialectica: who it is for, what value experts get back, what sessions should exist, what content should come out of them, and what success looks like from the community side.

I own turning that bet into a technical operating system. I would map the current workflow, define the data model, decide what should be built or bought, design the AI and review workflow, shape the first operator prototype, and define the technical metrics that show whether Delphi is compounding. I do not own legal approval, company sponsorship, or community strategy by myself.

Michael owns the company support layer. He or another named sponsor should define the technical bar, unblock access to systems and data, clarify what Dialectica considers core IP, and judge whether the first phase is strong enough to continue. If Michael is not that sponsor, the sponsor needs to be named.

## Conversation Targets

From Pere, I need to know the first expert/community loop, the first give/get offers, what "dbt -> emailing" means in real systems, and whether he wants an operator workflow before a member-facing platform.

From Michael, I need to know whether he is sponsor/evaluator, how he defines ROI for Delphi, what should be built versus bought, what data and systems I can inspect, and what decision rights come with the role.

From both, I need alignment that the first phase is discovery and thin prototyping, not premature platform delivery.

## Decision Rule

Accept serious negotiation if they engage with this charter at the right level.

Negotiate if the idea is strong but sponsorship, authority, access, or title are not written down.

Continue discovery if they like the direction but cannot name owners or first-phase commitments.

Decline or step back if they reframe the role as ordinary senior engineering delivery with strategic expectations attached.
