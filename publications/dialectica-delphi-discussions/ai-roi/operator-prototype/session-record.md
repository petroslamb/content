## Operating Read

This session is valuable because it moves the AI ROI conversation away from vendor claims and toward operating reality. The useful question is not whether AI helps people produce more code. The useful question is where the apparent productivity gain disappears before it becomes business value.

The room's strongest pattern is the separation between local acceleration and system throughput. Code generation may move faster, but review, QA, compliance, security, deployment, product adoption, token budgeting, and team design still determine whether the organization improves. That makes the session a good template for Delphi's "operating-truth" format: a private room where senior operators compare hidden bottlenecks and imperfect metrics.
