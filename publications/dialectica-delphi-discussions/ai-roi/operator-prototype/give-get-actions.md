# Give/Get Actions: AI ROI

## Follow-Up Principle

Do not ask participants for more AI ROI data without giving back something that helps them benchmark their own operating model. The strongest exchange is an anonymized peer metric map in return for a narrow bottleneck signal.

| ID | Segment | Give | Get | Channel | Owner | Success Signal |

## Draft Follow-Up Prompt

Subject: AI ROI follow-up: where does the gain disappear?

```text
Thank you for joining the Delphi AI ROI discussion.

We are preparing a short anonymized operating memo from the session. The strongest theme was that code generation is only one part of ROI; the gain often disappears later in review, QA, deployment, security, token governance, or adoption.

If you are open to it, reply with the one bottleneck that is most visible in your organization today:

1. Code generation
2. Review
3. QA/testing
4. Security/compliance
5. Deployment/release
6. Token spend/model governance
7. Adoption by teams or business users

We will use the responses only in aggregated form and share the anonymized pattern back with participants.
```
