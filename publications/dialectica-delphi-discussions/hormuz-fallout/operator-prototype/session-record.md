## Operating Read

This session is valuable because it demonstrates Delphi's ability to put multiple operational domains into one room. The opening frame is explicit: a Hormuz crisis cannot be understood only through geopolitics, oil, or defense. It has to be read as a chain reaction across tactical maritime reality, defense systems, insurance, treasury, energy, and supply-chain continuity.

The strongest read is that each domain changed the meaning of the previous one. Defense raised questions of escort, demining, counter-drone capacity, munitions supply, and real-time data integration. Insurance reframed disruption as delayed repricing, contract exposure, and organizational preparedness. Treasury turned rerouting and premiums into dynamic cash forecasting. Energy made the buffer question explicit: reserves and inventories can hide the shock for a while, but not indefinitely. Supply chain brought the discussion back to execution: alternative routes exist, but they are slower, more expensive, and harder for firms that stayed too lean.
