# Give/Get Actions: Hormuz Fallout

## Follow-Up Principle

The strongest post-session exchange is a cross-domain scenario map in return for one concrete planning assumption. Participants should be routed by the part of the chain they actually care about, not sent a generic recap.

## Draft Follow-Up Prompt

Subject: Hormuz follow-up: which part of the chain is your red line?

```text
Thank you for joining the Delphi Hormuz Fallout briefing.

We are preparing a no-attribution chain-reaction map from the session. The strongest pattern was that the crisis does not move through one channel. Tactical maritime disruption can reprice insurance, strain treasury, expose energy buffers, and force supply-chain changes.

If useful, reply with the part of the chain that matters most for your organization:

1. Defense / maritime operations
2. Insurance / contract exposure
3. Treasury / liquidity
4. Energy supply or transition
5. Supply-chain continuity
6. Procurement / local sourcing
7. Geopolitical strategy

We will use responses only in aggregated form unless you explicitly agree otherwise.
```
