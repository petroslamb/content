## Disagreement And Uncertainty

The session contains both operational claims and geopolitical interpretation. The operator workflow should separate them. The strongest reusable value is the cross-domain dependency map, not a definitive geopolitical forecast.

The most important uncertainty is timing. Several claims imply delayed effects: insurance repricing, energy buffer depletion, supplier stress, and contract renegotiation. Those should be tracked as scenarios rather than stated as certain outcomes.
