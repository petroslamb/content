## Promise

This pass keeps the same thesis and changes the public object. The essay still argues that workflow specifications become meaningful source-like artifacts for agent systems only when they are coupled to validation, policy enforcement, and semantic debugging traces. What changes is the entry and the pressure distribution. `r2` must stop opening as a taxonomy correction and start from a concrete workflow problem that shows why the specification layer is already acting like source. The local `spec-diff` proof has to move from late corroboration into the load-bearing middle, and the literature stack has to compress into fewer turns that the prose itself controls.

## Spine

1. Concrete opening problem: start from an operator-facing workflow moment where a human-readable spec surface is edited, validated, and shown to govern real behavior.
2. Source-like specification layer: name the shift from passive documentation to interpretable, constrained workflow artifacts.
3. Validation and policy hinge: show that validators and policy enforcement are what attach the spec layer to reality.
4. Trace and local proof section: use `spec-diff`, trace evaluation, and lifecycle validation to show why traces debug source-linked drift without becoming the source of truth.
5. Skills and bounded support layer: keep skills as one compressed support movement with routing and capability-binding caveats visible.
6. Provenance boundary and close: name claim-level provenance as the next missing layer and end by bounding the category rather than inflating it.

## Paragraph burden

- The opening must make the architectural stakes concrete before naming the slogan dispute.
- The specification section must explain what makes a workflow surface source-like without claiming markdown executes by itself.
- The validation section must make validation and policy load-bearing, not an afterthought.
- The trace section must bring the local proof earlier and keep the source/trace hierarchy explicit.
- The skills section must remain short and supportive.
- The ending must keep provenance as the forward boundary and preserve the essay's honesty about current repo limits.

## Failure triggers

- The draft opens with category correction instead of a concrete workflow pressure point.
- The middle reverts to paper parade rather than prose-led synthesis.
- The local `spec-diff` proof stays late and subordinate.
- The draft treats run traces as primary source rather than as debugging surfaces.
- The ending widens into claims about repo maturity or portable skills substrate that the evidence does not support.

## Opening Lab

Open from a bounded local workflow scene: a human edits the governing workflow surface, a validator catches drift, and the system reveals that the human-readable specification layer is already functioning like source. Only after that scene should the essay step back and reject the looser "markdown as code" slogan.

## Object Decision

No object change. Keep the essay about workflow specifications plus validation, policy, traces, and provenance pressure. Do not pivot into a generic context-engineering piece and do not reopen the package around skills.
