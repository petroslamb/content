## Whole-Draft Rationale

`r1` is a real essay candidate, not just a cleaned-up research brief. Its strongest quality is conceptual discipline: the draft keeps the architecture narrow, refuses markdown maximalism, and identifies provenance as the next missing layer instead of pretending the stack is already complete. The draft still falls below the stronger archive cluster because its opening stays meta and its middle still feels like a paper-led synthesis rather than a fully pressurized public argument.
