# Contradiction Register

This is the package-local contradiction surface for the Claim-State Authorization variant of the evidence authorization workflow.

It records only tensions that affect the article frame, proof boundary, or claim permission. It is not a full Evidence Governance import and should stay smaller than the claim ledger.

## Status Labels

- `resolved`: the authority rule is clear enough for drafting.
- `open`: the tension is real and needs more evidence, proof, or framing.
- `parked`: the tension is known but outside the current article or proof scope.
- `blocked`: the claim cannot be used until the tension is resolved.
