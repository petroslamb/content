# Pivot North-Star Viability

## Candidate

`The Real System Lives In The Contract Layer`

## Field-Reordering Move

The essay would argue that the real governing system in modern agent workflows has moved outward into human-readable contracts, policy surfaces, and evidence maps. That shifts the reader from "debug the runtime harder" to "inspect the contract layer that is already steering behavior."

## False Proxy Or Public Enemy

The public enemy is the advisory-prose illusion:

operators still read workflow specs, package manifests, policy surfaces, and evidence indexes as commentary even while runtimes, validators, and audits already treat them as governing artifacts.

That illusion creates the wrong debugging posture: runtime symptoms get attention after the contract layer has already drifted.

## Evidence Aperture

The evidence is strong on:

- natural-language harnesses and explicit workflow specification
- validation and policy enforcement acting on editable workflow surfaces
- trace-level checking and continuous validation loops
- the need for claim-level provenance after claims are promoted

The evidence is weaker on:

- field-scale consequence outside governed workflow systems
- broader non-repo examples where contract-layer drift clearly outruns runtime diagnosis
- a public-language term that travels cleanly beyond agent-workflow architecture readers

## Doctrine Or Operator Consequence

If this thesis is right, builders can no longer treat workflow contracts, policy files, and evidence surfaces as secondary documentation. They have to govern and debug them as the layer where authority, scope, policy, and evidence discipline are already being decided.

That is a real consequence, and the pressure pass improved it materially:

- contract drift becomes governance drift
- authority boundaries move before runtime checks
- cascading workflow failures become symptoms of upstream contract breakage
- trace-only accountability fails once promoted claims inherit drifted governance

Even so, it still lands more strongly as governed agent-systems doctrine than as a broader field-reordering public demand.

## North-Star Verdict

`not_north_star_viable`
