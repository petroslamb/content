# Pivot Grounding Review

## Purpose

This review records whether the current post-ceiling pivot candidates are grounded enough to justify opening a new flagship successor object.

The pass is deliberately constrained to tracked package state. It does not open a fresh alphaXiv search. It asks whether the donor package already contains one real successor seam before any new search effort is authorized.

## Method

The grounding pass reranked candidates against the package's own settled surfaces:

1. the canonical donor report
2. the terminal replacement review and decision
3. the claim ledger and contradiction register
4. the article-surface and provenance proof notes
5. the local `spec-diff` proof candidate and package-local validator surfaces

The goal was not to accumulate more papers. The goal was to decide whether one candidate family is already grounded enough to remain alive after the stop gate.

## Main Findings

1. The strongest grounded successor is not the old top-line frame about markdown becoming a programming substrate. It is the narrower doctrine that validation is the lifecycle infrastructure attaching workflow source to reality.
2. `You're Debugging The Wrong Layer` is the package's best surviving wrapper, not its best evidence seam. It remains useful as the accusation that opens or pressures the candidate, but it is not grounded enough to stand alone.
3. `Traces Are Not Provenance` is the strongest narrower synthesis. It survives as a supporting seam, not as the best flagship successor.
4. `Workflow Is A Namespace` remains bounded to navigation and routing. It does not yet justify a flagship pivot.
5. The validation candidate is already grounded enough to stay alive without another broad discovery pass. It is not yet strong enough to clear a north-star attempt.

## Grounding Verdict

`research_grounded_but_not_north_star_viable`

## Residual Risks

- The candidate may still collapse into infrastructure explanation rather than a larger public argument.
- The current evidence is stronger for engineering doctrine than for field-reordering consequence.
- The public enemy is still underpowered if it stays at "terminal QA" rather than a broader mistake about where workflow systems are actually governed.
- The package can still regress into a stack tour if validation, traces, and provenance are not subordinated to one doctrine thread.

## Bottom Line

Keep `Validation Is Lifecycle Infrastructure` as the active grounded candidate. Keep `You're Debugging The Wrong Layer` as a reusable wrapper only. Do not open a sibling flagship package yet. The next bounded move is the explicit north-star viability test for the grounded candidate.
