## Working Thesis

Once workflow specs, policy surfaces, and evidence indexes start governing agent behavior, validation stops being terminal QA. It becomes the lifecycle infrastructure that keeps those source-like surfaces attached to reality.

## Residual Risk

The strongest remaining risk is scale.

The seam is well-grounded as internal doctrine, but not yet clearly north-star viable as a flagship public object. The current evidence is stronger at proving:

- why validation belongs throughout the workflow lifecycle
- why runtime-only debugging is insufficient
- why traces and provenance belong in the same architecture

It is weaker at proving:

- that this seam alone reorders the broader field
- that the sharpened public enemy is large enough to anchor a top-cluster flagship rather than a strong doctrine essay
- that the operator consequence grows beyond "validate earlier and more often"

## Residual Gaps To Carry Into Research Delta

- stronger consequences outside repo-shaped workflow examples
- a cleaner argument for why lifecycle validation beats narrower framing like trace evaluation or provenance alone
- a way to keep the essay on one doctrine seam without quietly expanding back into the full stack tour

Treat this as the active grounded candidate, not as a cleared pivot.

Do not initialize a sibling flagship package yet. The next bounded move is to test north-star viability directly and keep the candidate blocked until it clears that bar.
