# Pivot Blocker

## Blocked Candidate

`Validation Is Lifecycle Infrastructure`

## Block Type

`north_star_failure`

## Why It Is Blocked

This candidate is real and well-supported, but it is now blocked as the package's pivot answer.

The issue is not quality. The issue is scale.

It proved strong enough to become a serious bounded doctrine object, but not large enough to justify the package's reopened flagship-successor search. Keeping it alive as the pivot answer would only rename a salvage route that the workflow has now removed.

## Evidence Used In The Block

- the donor ceiling report
- the archived validation-doctrine grounding review
- the archived north-star viability note
- the archived pivot decision
- the doctrine branch review trail

## Blocking Findings

1. The candidate survives as doctrine more easily than as a field-reordering flagship.
2. Its strongest consequence remains inside governed workflow engineering rather than escaping into a larger public argument.
3. Its best opener still borrows force from the larger wrong-layer accusation and contract-governance seam.

## What Would Unblock It

At least one of these would have to arrive:

- a broader governing term than validation alone
- a consequence surface that clearly outruns workflow-engineering doctrine
- evidence that the doctrine is actually the visible face of a larger governance mistake

## Workflow Consequence

Do not treat `Validation Is Lifecycle Infrastructure` as the active successor candidate.

Preserve it as a historical exploratory branch and transfer only what helps the larger restarted search.
