# Research Exit Gate

## Governing Blueprint

The governing brief is [`research-handoff.md`](research-handoff.md), reinforced by the active pivot decision and north-star viability gate. Together they define the new object: controlled, maintained, typed, linted, validated, or trace-linked specification surfaces becoming source-of-truth artifacts in AI-native development. The handoff also supplies the kill boundary: do not claim `natural language is code`.

The capped donor blueprint remains useful only as shared prior discipline: source/runtime hierarchy, validation pressure, trace boundaries, and parked overclaims. It is not the successor thesis by itself.

## Evidence Surface

The machine-resolved evidence surface is the shared [`../papers/`](../papers/) library and [`../papers/index.md`](../papers/index.md). It already contains the donor source/runtime/validation/trace stack: specification-as-program, natural-language harnesses, AgentSPEX, test-driven agent definition, ContextCov, policy enforcement, trace compliance, validation loops, and claim-level auditability.

The successor also inherits an auxiliary surface from [`../harness_papers/index.md`](../harness_papers/index.md), the reopened specification-surface pressure test, and the pivot grounding review. Those surfaces are sufficient for a bounded essay-iteration packet because they name the active claim set, the public enemy, the allowed synthesis, and the caveats. The non-agent bridge papers remain follow-on triggers for quote-level body use, not blockers to opening a bounded candidate.

## Claim Boundaries

The claim-boundary surface is [`specification-surface-source-of-truth-claim-boundary-ledger.md`](specification-surface-source-of-truth-claim-boundary-ledger.md).

It authorizes the largest claim only in its narrowed form: durable authority increasingly shifts toward maintained specification surfaces where agents generate, revise, test, or coordinate implementation. It blocks the tempting larger claims that ordinary prose is code, that generated implementation no longer matters, or that context files are automatically beneficial.

The ledger also keeps three caveats attached to drafting: context-file evidence is metric-dependent, specification formality is not automatic conformance, and current local proof artifacts are bounded drift/debugging surfaces rather than full semantic provenance graphs.

## Drafting Boundary

`essay_iteration` may start from this claim set:

- specification surfaces are the governing object;
- `vibe` is the false proxy;
- controlled, maintained, validated surfaces can become source-of-truth artifacts;
- traces and generated outputs remain derived debugging surfaces;
- context files and controlled natural language are bridge evidence only when scoped and caveated.

Remaining research is conditional draft pressure. If the candidate leans heavily on the non-agent bridge papers, those papers need direct inspection or intake before final promotion. If the candidate starts sounding like `natural language is code`, the draft should be cut or the research gate reopened.

The successor leaf has enough local evidence, pivot pressure, and claim-boundary control to seed a bounded essay-iteration packet.
