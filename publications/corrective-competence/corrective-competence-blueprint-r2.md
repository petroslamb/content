## Promise

This pass will keep the public object stable and tighten the synthesis claim. The essay will argue that the cross-domain invariant is not generic deskilling, but loss of maintenance competence: once AI becomes the first generator of candidate moves and the human shifts into evaluating machine proposals, the skills needed to detect and repair errors decay faster than assisted output improves. The frame still survives. The only question is whether the draft can make that invariant explicit enough to earn the synthesis.

## Spine

1. Keep the persistence opener but add concrete task texture.
2. Define corrective competence quickly.
3. State the cross-domain invariant explicitly.
4. Rebuild the mechanism section around skill formation, epistemic debt, and the formal competence-shadow turn.
5. Keep calibration as a bounded rival hinge.
6. End with a domain-conditional delegation doctrine.

## Paragraph burden

- Opening: vivid anomaly, no abstract setup.
- Definition: name the concept without overselling its scope.
- Invariant paragraph: specify exactly what travels across domains.
- Mechanism section: show compounding degradation rather than a loose pile of harms.
- Rival hinge: concede calibration without surrendering the object.
- Ending: keep the doctrine concrete and proportional to domain stakes.
