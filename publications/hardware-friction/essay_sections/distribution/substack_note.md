# Substack Note: The Transformer Attractor

**Post link:** https://open.substack.com/pub/lambpetros/p/the-transformer-attractor

---

NVIDIA's 3-year roadmap includes a "Second-Generation Transformer Engine" and "3x faster Transformer layer performance."

What's conspicuously absent: any silicon support for parallel scans, recurrence units, or the primitives that alternative architectures actually need.

That's not neutral infrastructure investment. That's a $30B bet that Transformers remain dominant through 2028. And that bet shapes what architectures can even be attempted at frontier scale.

New essay on why every "Transformer killer" either converges toward Transformer compute patterns or dies at research scale: https://open.substack.com/pub/lambpetros/p/the-transformer-attractor
