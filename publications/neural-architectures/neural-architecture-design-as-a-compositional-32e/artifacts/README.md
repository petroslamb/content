# Artifacts

Local artifact surface for media or publication assets that belong specifically to the `neural-architecture-design-as-a-compositional-32e` leaf package.

Keep shared umbrella evidence in [`../../papers/`](../../papers/) and keep only slug-specific artifacts here.

Current local split:

- `audio/`: narration exports and transcript recovery
- `slides/`: exported slide deck, semantically named kept visuals, and raw numbered slide images
- `video/`: captions, cue sheets, long-form renders, and Shorts derivatives
- `flashcards/` and `quiz/`: NotebookLM study artifacts

The semantically named top-level slide images are the editorially kept article visuals. The numbered slide tree under `slides/the_neural_scaffold_detailed/` remains the raw production deck used for video work.
