# <Package Or Essay> Contradiction Register

## Purpose

This register records only high-value tensions that affect claim permission, proof requirements, or article framing.

It is not a general objection list. Add a tension only when resolving it would change the claim ledger, scorecard, blueprint, or draft instruction.

## Status Labels

- `resolved`: the authority rule is clear enough for drafting.
- `open`: the tension is real and needs more evidence, proof, or framing.
- `parked`: the tension is known but outside the current article or proof scope.
- `blocked`: the claim cannot be used until the tension is resolved.
