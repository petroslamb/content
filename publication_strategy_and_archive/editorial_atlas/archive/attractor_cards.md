# Attractor Cards

Short cards for every archive essay.

Use these when a new draft needs a pull essay quickly and the full atlas feels
too abstract.

## The Transformer Attractor

- Identity: flagship contrarian thesis essay
- Best use: opener, thesis compression, ending force
- Signature strength: turns a field pattern into one portable structural claim
- Overpull risk: can make a weaker evidence base sound more settled than it is
- Best borrow: cold open + final landing

## The Binding Gap

- Identity: coined concept / precision essay
- Best use: naming, borders, exact conceptual framing
- Signature strength: introduces a term without letting it become vague
- Overpull risk: too much caution can narrow the public object
- Best borrow: borders section

## The Autonomy Tax

- Identity: public framework / operator essay
- Best use: practical doctrine with public energy
- Signature strength: vivid failure open converted into an actionable frame
- Overpull risk: managerial structure can flatten voice
- Best borrow: incident-led opener and doctrine close

## The Illusion of the Swarm

- Identity: balanced synthesis / builder essay
- Best use: research-heavy builder pieces that still need a sharp claim
- Signature strength: caveated contrarianism without losing usefulness
- Overpull risk: too much qualification can soften the edge
- Best borrow: middle architecture

## What Actually Works

- Identity: long-form evidence-backed survey
- Best use: archive anchor for large evidence syntheses
- Signature strength: converts a large source layer into a clear organizing lens
- Overpull risk: front matter and scale can delay ignition
- Best borrow: evidence-backed development section

## The Moltbook Phenomenon

- Identity: topical narrative intervention
- Best use: essays built on a current event or strange public object
- Signature strength: narrative propulsion from the very first lines
- Overpull risk: topicality expires faster than framework pieces
- Best borrow: topical opener

## The Hardware Friction Map

- Identity: practitioner heuristic essay
- Best use: short practical companion to a bigger thesis
- Signature strength: compresses a large architecture story into usable filters
- Overpull risk: may feel derivative if it does not lean on a larger frame
- Best borrow: practical heuristic section

## Autonomous AI Agents: Core Foundations and Recent Breakthroughs

- Identity: large survey / reading-guide essay
- Best use: building archive map pieces or field primers
- Signature strength: breadth and navigability across a large paper set
- Overpull risk: chapterization can dilute the central thesis
- Best borrow: research-map framing

## The Orchestration Paradigm Series

- Identity: series architecture / portal essay
- Best use: introducing a long publication line
- Signature strength: packages a deep series into a readable route
- Overpull risk: package scaffolding can outweigh local essay force
- Best borrow: reader map

## The Orchestration Paradigm: Issue 4 - The Reality

- Identity: production payoff / late-series reality check
- Best use: final-stage payoff essays in a series
- Signature strength: shifts a technical series toward stakes and deployment
- Overpull risk: relies on earlier setup for full force
- Best borrow: payoff section

## When the LLM Programs Its Own Thinking

- Identity: technical builder essay
- Best use: pieces where implementation detail is part of the argument
- Signature strength: concrete examples make a technical frame legible
- Overpull risk: can taper into documentation instead of landing as an essay
- Best borrow: worked example

## Operating Agents Guide: Preface

- Identity: package contract / doctrine note
- Best use: prefaces, course intros, package-level doctrine
- Signature strength: states the publication standard clearly and compactly
- Overpull risk: not built as a standalone flagship object
- Best borrow: doctrine close
