# Move Library

Named editorial moves reverse-engineered from the published archive.

Each move should stay anchored to actual essays. The archive matters here more
than the abstraction.

## 1. Failure or Anomaly Cold Open

Open with a concrete failure, anomaly, or contradiction that immediately makes
the thesis feel necessary.

- Best archive examples:
  - `The Transformer Attractor`
  - `The Autonomy Tax`
  - `The Binding Gap`
  - `The Moltbook Phenomenon`
- Best use:
  - flagship public essays
  - contrarian framing
  - concept-launch pieces
- Overuse risk:
  - can become formulaic if the anomaly is decorative rather than load-bearing

## 2. Contrarian Compression

State the essay's whole dispute in one portable line early enough that it can
travel outside the piece.

- Best archive examples:
  - `The Transformer Attractor`
  - `The Illusion of the Swarm`
  - `The Autonomy Tax`
- Best use:
  - essays intended for broad discussion or reposting
- Overuse risk:
  - a compressed thesis can outrun the actual evidence base

## 3. Coined Frame With Borders

Introduce a new term, but immediately narrow what it does and does not include.

- Best archive examples:
  - `The Binding Gap`
  - `The Autonomy Tax`
  - `The Hardware Friction Map`
- Best use:
  - concept pieces
  - evaluation or diagnosis essays
- Overuse risk:
  - can become taxonomy-first and lose public energy

## 4. Scroll Map / Reader Map

Make the route explicit before the heavy material begins.

- Best archive examples:
  - `What Actually Works`
  - `Autonomous AI Agents: Core Foundations and Recent Breakthroughs`
  - `The Orchestration Paradigm Series`
- Best use:
  - long pieces
  - series entries
  - educational objects
- Overuse risk:
  - too much upfront scaffolding can delay ignition

## 5. Builder-First Doctrine

Translate research into a conservative build order rather than a hype summary.

- Best archive examples:
  - `The Autonomy Tax`
  - `The Illusion of the Swarm`
  - the `Operating Agents` chapters
- Best use:
  - operator-facing essays
  - practical architecture pieces
- Overuse risk:
  - can flatten voice into sober guidance only

## 6. Historical Synthesis

Turn many papers or stages into one long argument about how the field moved.

- Best archive examples:
  - `What Actually Works`
  - `Autonomous AI Agents: Core Foundations and Recent Breakthroughs`
  - `The Illusion of the Swarm`
- Best use:
  - long-form surveys
  - field-setting essays
- Overuse risk:
  - synthesis drag; momentum collapses if the line of motion is weak

## 7. Practical Heuristic Section

Insert a section that gives the reader a usable filter, scorecard, or Monday
rule.

- Best archive examples:
  - `The Hardware Friction Map`
  - `The Autonomy Tax`
  - `The Illusion of the Swarm`
- Best use:
  - operator or strategy pieces
- Overuse risk:
  - can feel bolted on if the essay did not earn the heuristic

## 8. Falsifiable Prediction Close

End with a prediction that clarifies what would disprove the thesis.

- Best archive examples:
  - `The Transformer Attractor`
  - `The Autonomy Tax`
  - `The Illusion of the Swarm`
- Best use:
  - essays making strong structural claims
- Overuse risk:
  - prediction theater if the criteria are vague

## 9. Topical Narrative Hijack

Use a current event or news object to smuggle in a deeper structural argument.

- Best archive examples:
  - `The Moltbook Phenomenon`
- Best use:
  - fast interventions
  - product or platform essays
- Overuse risk:
  - shelf life shortens unless the deeper frame is genuinely portable

## 10. Series Packaging

Treat the essay as one node inside a larger designed reading path.

- Best archive examples:
  - `The Orchestration Paradigm Series`
  - the `Operating Agents` corpus
- Best use:
  - courses
  - staged publication lines
- Overuse risk:
  - the local essay becomes dependent on package context

## 11. Chapterized Technical Explainer

Use chapter logic, explicit part labels, and annotated references to make dense
material navigable.

- Best archive examples:
  - `Autonomous AI Agents: Core Foundations and Recent Breakthroughs`
  - the `Orchestration Paradigm` issues
  - `Operating Agents`
- Best use:
  - teaching and archive objects
- Overuse risk:
  - public-object strength weakens if the piece reads like internal notes

## 12. Doctrine Ending

Close by stating what the publication stands for or how the reader should build
differently.

- Best archive examples:
  - `The Autonomy Tax`
  - `Operating Agents Guide: Preface`
  - `The Illusion of the Swarm`
- Best use:
  - package-defining essays
  - framework pieces
- Overuse risk:
  - can become sermon-like without enough preceding evidence

## Fast Pairings

Useful move combinations already present in the archive:

- `Failure Cold Open + Coined Frame`: `The Binding Gap`
- `Failure Cold Open + Contrarian Compression + Prediction Close`: `The Transformer Attractor`
- `Incident Open + Practical Heuristic + Doctrine Ending`: `The Autonomy Tax`
- `Contrarian Compression + Historical Synthesis + Builder Doctrine`: `The Illusion of the Swarm`
- `Topical Narrative Hijack + Structural Reframe`: `The Moltbook Phenomenon`
