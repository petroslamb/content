# Section Atlas

First-pass section-level scoring across the archive corpus.

## Columns

- `Opening`: attention, stakes, and early propulsion
- `Thesis Lock`: where the claim becomes stable and portable
- `Development`: middle architecture and sustained force
- `Borders`: limits, objections, and scope control
- `Ending`: closure, payoff, and memorability
- `Reference Handling`: source handling inside the essay object
- `Power Source`: where most of the essay's force comes from
- `Best Borrow`: section or move another draft can safely borrow

## Practical Use

This table is more useful for revision than the whole-essay atlas.

Examples:

- If a draft needs a better opener, borrow from `Transformer Attractor`, `Autonomy Tax`, or `Moltbook`, not from the whole essay shape.
- If a draft needs a stronger thesis lock, borrow from `Transformer Attractor` or `Binding Gap`.
- If a draft needs stronger borders, borrow from `Binding Gap` or `Operating Agents IV`.
- If a draft needs a stronger research-backed middle, borrow from `Illusion of the Swarm` or `What Actually Works`.
