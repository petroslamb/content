## Early Editorial Read

The archive is not dominated by one mode. It contains at least five distinct
editorial object types:

- flagship thesis essays
- coined-concept essays
- long evidence-backed syntheses
- topical narrative interventions
- builder-first course chapters

That diversity is useful. It means a new essay does not need to imitate the
current flagship shape by default.
