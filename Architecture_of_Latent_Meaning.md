### 4. Takeaway 3: The End of Reading—DNA as Visual Semantics
The "DNA Semantics" project by Gregor Mobius provides the "yes-proof" for visual reasoning. Mobius replaced the traditional, discrete symbolic letters of genetic code (A, C, G, T, U) with a grayscale visual mapping: **U (Black) → C (Dark Gray) → A (Medium Gray) → G (Light Gray) → T (White)**.

By converting categorical symbols into ordered values, complex biological information becomes a "D-Icon"—a semantic compression unit where the viewer perceives the "texture" of the sequence simultaneously rather than reading it linearly. Through this lens, "Entropy" is immediately legible: a high-entropy sequence (like ATA/TAT) appears as a chaotic, flickering checkerboard, while low entropy (like AAT/ATT) manifests as solid, uniform blocks. This confirms that intelligence can be a matter of pattern recognition rather than symbolic decoding. As Mobius observes:

> "Biology doesn’t speak. Physics doesn’t write. Neural systems don’t explain. They configure."
