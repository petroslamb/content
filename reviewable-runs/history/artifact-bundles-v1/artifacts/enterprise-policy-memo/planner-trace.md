# Planner Trace: Enterprise Policy Memo

## Step 1: Bound the task

- goal: draft an internal memo, not update policy text
- allowed actions: retrieve documents, extract evidence, draft memo, run verification
- blocked actions: publish, email, or change policy records without approval

## Step 2: Retrieve evidence

- fetch `POL-SEC-014` revision metadata and retention clauses
- fetch `POL-VEND-009` clauses on approved vendor classes and escalation requirements
- fetch `KB-CX-221` workflow notes for support-summary field definitions

## Step 3: Draft typed memo sections

- section 1: scope of AI-generated support summaries
- section 2: retention duration and deletion triggers
- section 3: vendor sharing rules and approved conditions
- section 4: approval path for new vendor exposure
- section 5: operational notes for support teams

## Step 4: Verify before approval

- confirm each factual claim has a source row
- confirm no superseded revision was cited
- confirm the memo does not instruct publication or external sharing

## Step 5: Hold at approval gate

- present memo plus evidence record
- request compliance approval before posting to the policy hub
