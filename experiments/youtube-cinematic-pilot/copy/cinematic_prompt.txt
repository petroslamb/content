Create a documentary-style video overview for a Rooted Layers publication.

Prioritize structural change, causality, and long-term implications over recap. Open from a concrete tension, failure chain, or decisive architectural shift. Keep the voice analytical, sober, and visually ambitious.

Treat this as a discovery artifact for viewers who do not know the essay yet. The video should stand on its own, but it should clearly reward a click through to the full research breakdown afterward.

Build the narration in compact claim blocks that can be clipped cleanly into 25-45 second Shorts without extra setup. Include at least three self-contained lines or mini-sequences that each:
- open with a sharp claim, tension, or failure mode in the first sentence
- avoid pronoun-heavy callbacks that require earlier context to understand
- land one concrete takeaway before moving on
- sound quotable when isolated from the rest of the video

Prefer crisp causal statements over layered explanation. If a section starts to feel like an essay paragraph, compress it until it can survive as a standalone clip.

Avoid listicle framing, generic YouTube hype, creator self-reference, and filler transitions. Do not use phrases like "TL;DR", "top 5", or "in this video". Favor memorable causal statements over enumerated summaries.
