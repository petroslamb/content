Draft references comment for: Why Most Multi-Agent AI Is a Workaround

This brief leans most heavily on these sources:

- **When Single-Agent with Skills Replace Multi-Agent Systems and When They Fail** — the anchor paper behind the claim that many multi-agent stacks are compensating for routing and tool-selection problems rather than adding indispensable intelligence.
- **Towards a Science of Scaling Agent Systems** — useful here because it shows the effect of agent count and topology is conditional, not automatically positive.
- **Toward Efficient Agents: Memory, Tool Learning, and Planning** — supports the broader view that better memory, routing, and planning often matter more than simply adding more agent personas.
- **ROMA** — the strongest counterexample in the set because it shows real gains when decomposition is genuinely load-bearing rather than decorative.
- **SkillOrchestra** — reinforces the idea that explicit skill routing is often more valuable than vague multi-agent roleplay.
