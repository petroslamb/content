Draft references comment for: Why Meta Bought a Broken AI Social Network

This brief is based less on one single paper and more on a cluster of reporting and research sources:

- **Wiz’s Moltbook breach report** — the key technical source for the claim that the platform was catastrophically insecure, with exposed credentials and writable production data.
- **OpenClaw / Moltbook / ClawdLab research paper** — important because it treats Moltbook as a real in-the-wild lab for autonomous agents rather than just a viral curiosity.
- **Scott Alexander’s Moltbook notes** — useful for the “AI theater” framing and for separating genuine agent behavior from human-forced spectacle.
- **AP and other acquisition reporting on Meta / OpenClaw** — supports the competitive context around Meta, OpenAI, and the strategic value of the agent graph.
- **Meta restructuring and acquisition reporting** — supports the internal-politics argument that the deal was partly about power inside Meta, not just product strategy.
