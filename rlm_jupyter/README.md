# RLM Jupyter

Research and production artifacts for *When the LLM Programs Its Own Thinking* (January 2026), exploring how LLMs can operate as shared-state REPL partners within a Jupyter environment.

## Contents

- `rlm-jupyter-integration.md` — Full research draft and article
- `rlm_paper.md` / `rlm_paper.pdf` — The companion research paper
- `RLM_Shared_State_in_Jupyter.pdf` — Slide deck for the visual overview
- `papers/` — Supporting research papers
- `visuals/` — Diagrams and visual assets
- `rlm-jupyter-integration-*.md` — Platform-specific promotional posts (HN, LinkedIn, Reddit, Twitter, YouTube)
