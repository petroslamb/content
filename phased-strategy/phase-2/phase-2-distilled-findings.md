# Phase 2 Distilled Findings

## Purpose

This file is the compact decision surface between the raw research inventory in [`idea-bank.md`](idea-bank.md) and the Phase 2 decision filters in [`claim-ledger.md`](claim-ledger.md) and [`contradiction-register.md`](contradiction-register.md).

It keeps only findings that materially affect Phase 2 now. It is not a replacement for the idea bank, and it is not itself the operative plan. Its job is to reduce how often Phase 2 planning needs to consult the full upstream inventory.

## Authority Position

Use the Phase 2 planning stack in this order:

1. [`idea-bank.md`](idea-bank.md) is the raw upstream research surface.
2. `phase-2-distilled-findings.md` is the latest compact distillation of findings that matter now.
3. [`claim-ledger.md`](claim-ledger.md) and [`contradiction-register.md`](contradiction-register.md) decide what can be promoted and what authority rule constrains it.
4. [`architecture.md`](architecture.md), [`milestones.md`](milestones.md), [`workflow-yaml-schema.md`](workflow-yaml-schema.md), [`2.1-executable-workflow-contract.md`](2.1-executable-workflow-contract.md), [`2.2-enforcement-and-validation-layer.md`](2.2-enforcement-and-validation-layer.md), and [`2.3-trace-and-provenance-minimums.md`](2.3-trace-and-provenance-minimums.md) are the current operative plan.
5. [`open-questions.md`](open-questions.md) is the unresolved sidecar for questions that can block, narrow, or defer promotion.

Do not promote a finding from this file directly into implementation. First pass it through the claim ledger and contradiction register.

## Promotion Status Labels

- `Adopted for Phase 2 planning`: strong enough to update or strengthen downstream planning docs after the claim and contradiction layer is revised.
- `Needs pressure before promotion`: likely important, but the boundary, storage surface, proof requirement, or authority rule is not yet settled.
- `Pressure only`: useful caution or design pressure, but not a Phase 2 rewrite driver.
- `Parked`: known but out of scope unless a concrete Phase 2 failure mode appears.
