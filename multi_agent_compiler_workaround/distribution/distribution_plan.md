## Engagement Rules

1. Reply early, but do not argue with everyone.
2. Keep the claims narrow. "Many same-model stacks are compensating for routing and state problems" is stronger than "multi-agent is over."
3. On Reddit, never push the link twice in the same thread.
4. If someone wants implementation detail, point them to the papers before the article when appropriate.
5. If a mod or active commenter pushes back on promotion format, comply immediately and do not debate moderation in-thread.
