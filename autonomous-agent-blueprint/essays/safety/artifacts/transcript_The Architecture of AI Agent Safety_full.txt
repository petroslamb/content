Because LLMs are inherently psychopantic and easily swayed by confident sounding language. Having two vulnerable language models argue about safety is useless. if neither of them is restrained by hard codebased access restrictions that physically prevent the deletion.



That makes a lot of sense. The logic layer is too squishy to be the final line of defense. Lastly, the blueprint says, "Do not treat post hawk monitoring as a substitute for active containment."



Watching a beautiful data dashboard to see if your agent just hallucinated and executed a massive erroneous stock trade is not helpful.
