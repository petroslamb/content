## North Star

This project should be treated as an ideas essay project.

An ideas essay is a piece whose primary unit of value is the conceptual move.

More explicitly: it introduces, sharpens, or reorders a way of seeing a problem, so that the reader leaves with a better mental model than they had before. Its job is not mainly to report facts, summarize literature, or argue a policy preference. Its job is to make an idea feel necessary.

In practice, that means an ideas essay is judged mostly on:

- Whether the thesis is genuinely clarifying or novel
- Whether the core concepts are defined cleanly
- Whether the idea has explanatory power across real cases
- Whether the boundaries and limits are honestly stated
- Whether the reader's frame changes after reading it

What it is not:

- Not a research paper: it does not need formal proof or exhaustive evidence
- Not a literature review: sources support the idea, they are not the main event
- Not an opinion column: the claim has to do real conceptual work, not just assert attitude
- Not a trend piece: novelty in topic is not enough

Working standard: the essay succeeds when it gives the reader a new lens that is precise enough to use, strong enough to survive skepticism, and generative enough to explain more than one example. If it only says something interesting, it is decent. If it changes the reader's operating vocabulary, it is excellent.
